Research Experience

Project TeLI (National Science Foundation)

2026–present Research Assistant, supervised by Dr. Hadley (PI) · Tampa, FL

A three-year NSF-funded collaboration among the University of South Florida, the University of Texas at Dallas, and the University of Florida that uses wearable LENA audio recorders, Bluetooth proximity sensors, and automatic speech recognition to study teacher–child language interactions in pre-K classrooms.

  • Science talk coding. Hand-code classroom transcripts for science content talk (e.g., living things, materials, forces, weather) and science practice talk (e.g., observing, predicting, testing, explaining), with confidence ratings, as one of two independent coders. The coded data are used to train a large language model to identify science talk automatically across the full dataset.
  • Interaction coding. Coded audio-aligned ASR transcripts from 16 classrooms to identify teacher–child conversational interactions and focal-child interactions, treating audio as the ground truth, to train algorithms that detect classroom interactions automatically. Documented coding decisions to support inter-rater reliability checks.

Linguistic Synchrony BIS Project

2026–present Research Assistant, supervised by Dr. Sara Smith · Tampa, FL

Conducted systematic coding of teacher utterances from BIS (Behavioral Interaction System) transcripts to support research on linguistic and semantic synchrony in classroom interactions. Applied a structured coding framework to classify teachers’ pedagogical intentions, ensuring consistency and reliability through iterative validation and correction. Contributed to a research dataset linking instructional discourse with measures of semantic synchrony.
