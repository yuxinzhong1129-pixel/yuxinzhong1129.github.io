About

About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the College of Education at the University of South Florida, concentrating in Technology in Education and Second Language Acquisition (SLA).

My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, language pedagogy, and human-computer interaction. I am particularly interested in how intelligent systems can provide meaningful, formative feedback to English language learners, especially Chinese EFL learners, in ways that traditional instruction cannot.

Before USF, I earned my M.A. in TESOL from New York University.

⭐ Featured Project
SpeakPrecise
AI-Powered Speaking Practice for Chinese EFL Learners

SpeakPrecise is a web application I designed and built to help Chinese EFL learners improve their lexical precision in spoken English. It uses a rule-based NLP engine grounded in my published research to compute a real-time Lexical Imprecision Rate (LIR) score after each spoken or typed turn.

The app transcribes learner speech, identifies vague or over-general vocabulary, highlights problematic words, and suggests precise alternatives — all running entirely offline with no API dependency.
